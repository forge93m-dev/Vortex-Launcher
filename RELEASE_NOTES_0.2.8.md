# Vortex Launcher 0.2.8 — Vortex Client 0.2.4: animated capes online

## Vortex Client 0.2.4

- **Animated capes are now shared online.** The cape service lists seven capes (Vortex, Aurora, Ember and the
  new animated Nebula, Flow and Pulse). Players who choose an animated cape publish it, and other Vortex Client
  players see it. Older clients ignore capes they do not know and simply show no cape for them.
- Everything from 0.2.3 (party chat, shared waypoints, chat tabs, panorama picker, zoom).
- Existing instances: use **Update Vortex Client**. Worlds, mods and settings are preserved.

## Launcher

- No other launcher changes since 0.2.7.

## Notes

- Only Minecraft 26.3 is the main target; 26.1, 26.1.1, 26.1.2, 26.2 and 1.21.11 stay experimental.
- Checked by the client's mixin tests on every version; not played through on every version.
